Common Questions

The important stuff.

Is it really free?

Yes. Spaza Stock is free to use with no subscription, no ads and no hidden fees.

Do I need internet?

No. Estolo is designed to work offline. You can record sales and manage your stock without mobile data.

Can I add my own products?

Yes. Add your own products such as Coke, bread, milk, chips, airtime and anything else you sell.

Where is my data stored?

Your shop data currently stays on your phone. There is no account or cloud storage required.

What if I change phones?

Your current version stores data locally on the phone. There is no automatic transfer to a new phone.
